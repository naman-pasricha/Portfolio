About

The Person Behind the Work

I'm Naman Pasricha – a strategy-minded professional sitting at the intersection of data, business, and communication. Based in India, I work with numbers that tell stories, words that drive decisions, and ideas that actually get implemented.

My career has taken me across three distinct disciplines – data analytics, business development, and content strategy – and I've found that the most interesting work happens where all three overlap. Whether I'm building a financial dashboard in Power BI, crafting a client proposal at ATFS, or writing a case study on Starbucks' fintech evolution, the underlying goal is always the same: turn complexity into clarity.

How I got here

I started in content – leading a team of 5 at Skill91. That experience taught me how to communicate ideas that stick. From there, I moved into data analytics, interning at Afame Technologies and MeriSKILL, where I built dashboards, cleaned pipelines, and learned how to let data do the talking. And Then, as a Business Development Associate at AfterThought Feedback Services, I bring both of those worlds together – using research and analysis to open doors, and clear communication to walk through them.

Beyond the work

That curiosity is what drives my writing, and it's what keeps me sharp as an analyst. When I'm not working, you'll find me behind a camera, on a stage, or deep in a conversation about something that probably should've been a podcast.
